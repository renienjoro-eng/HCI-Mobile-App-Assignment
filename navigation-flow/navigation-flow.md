# Navigation Flow & Information Architecture

## 1. Overview

The University Student Portal is designed to give students quick access to academic and administrative services.

The information architecture is divided into three levels:

* **Authentication** – Splash Screen and Login
* **Primary Navigation** – Home, Courses, Fees and More
* **Secondary Screens** – Profile, Notifications, Settings, Course Details and other task-specific screens

The structure allows users to move from general sections to more specific functions without making the navigation complicated.

---

## 2. Primary Navigation

The application uses four main navigation destinations:

| Section     | Purpose                                          |
| ----------- | ------------------------------------------------ |
| **Home**    | Dashboard and quick access to important services |
| **Courses** | View and manage courses                          |
| **Fees**    | View fee and payment information                 |
| **More**    | Access Profile, Notifications and Settings       |

These four sections form the main navigation because they represent the major areas of the student portal.

---

## 3. Screen Hierarchy

The application follows a hierarchical structure.

The student first enters through the **Splash Screen**, proceeds to **Login**, and then reaches the **Home Dashboard**.

From the main application, users can access the primary sections and then move into more specific screens.

For example:

**Courses → My Courses → Course Details**

This keeps related information grouped together and makes the application easier to understand.

---

## 4. Main User Flow

The general user journey is:

**Splash → Login → Home → Primary Service → Task Completion**

After logging in, the student can choose the service they need without having to navigate through unnecessary screens.

The Home screen also acts as a central point for accessing important services such as course registration, academic results and profile information.

---

## 5. Course Registration Flow

Course registration is the main task-based flow in the application.

The student:

1. Opens **Course Registration**
2. Selects a course
3. Reviews the selection
4. Confirms the registration
5. Receives a registration confirmation
6. Views the registered course under **My Courses**

This follows a simple:

**Select → Review → Confirm → Complete**

pattern.

The confirmation step gives the user clear feedback that the task has been successfully completed.

---

## 6. Navigation Principles

The navigation structure follows four main principles:

* **Simplicity** – important services are easy to find.
* **Consistency** – navigation remains familiar across screens.
* **Clarity** – related features are grouped together.
* **Feedback** – important actions provide confirmation.

---

## 7. Summary

The information architecture organizes the Student Portal into a simple hierarchy.

The student moves from:

**Authentication → Home → Primary Service → Task Completion**

The four main navigation areas — **Home, Courses, Fees and More** — provide access to the major services, while secondary screens provide more detailed functionality.

The goal is to ensure that users always understand **where they are, what they can do next, and how to move back through the application.**
